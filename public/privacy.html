Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Quiet Marble handles information when you play at quietmarblegames.com or in the Quiet Marble mobile app for iPhone, iPad and Android. You do not need a game account. The website and the app share the same games and leaderboards; the mobile app and advertising sections explain where the app differs.

Game data and browser storage

A first-party anonymous player cookie connects ranked rounds to the same browser and expires after one year. Local storage keeps your last 100 saved results, sound preference and chosen light/dark theme. Session storage remembers your optional nickname for the tab session. You can clear these through your browser settings; the game's Privacy dialog also lets you clear local results.

Clearing browser data resets your local identity and history, but does not delete scores already submitted to the server. There is no account recovery or cross-device profile.

Public leaderboard and retention

We store an anonymous player identifier, round identifier, game, start time, optional nickname, and result metrics to validate and rank scores. Your qualifying nickname (or generated player label), moves, time, and result appear publicly. Use a nickname rather than personal information. Names are not unique or verified.

The server retains up to 100 completed results per game or marble board variant, with one best result per anonymous player. Superseded and lower-ranked scores are removed during cleanup. Unsubmitted rounds expire after 24 hours and are deleted on the next leaderboard read, game start, or score submission. Starting another round replaces your unfinished round for that game. A qualifying score and its nickname can remain until replaced or removed.

Nickname checks and name reports

The server rejects nicknames that contain offensive words, web addresses or names that imitate Quiet Marble or its staff. Names already on a leaderboard that fail these checks are shown as the anonymous player label instead.

Players can report another player's nickname from the app's leaderboards. A report stores the reported player's anonymous identifier, the reporter's anonymous identifier, the leaderboard, the reported name and the time; it does not store IP addresses or contact details. When three different players report a name, it is hidden automatically and replaced with the anonymous player label; we may also review reports and hide or restore names. Hiding a name never removes the score. Reports are deleted after 30 days, or sooner when we act on them. A player can send up to 20 reports a day.

Local storage also remembers whether you have seen the game hint explanation, so the popup is shown only once in this browser. Clearing site data resets this preference.

Sudoku streaks

For Sudoku, tab session storage keeps the current streak identifier and entered numbers so a refresh can resume your progress. The server keeps one current Sudoku streak per anonymous player, including the puzzle, starting difficulty, consecutive wins, total solving time, nickname and whether hints made the streak practice-only. Inactive streaks expire after 24 hours and are deleted on the next Sudoku start, update or leaderboard request. Only the top 100 players’ best streaks are retained publicly, showing nickname, wins, total time and starting difficulty. Starting a new streak replaces current progress but preserves a qualifying best score.

Recent website visitors

The counter estimates anonymous browsers viewing the game page in the last five minutes, including visitors who have not started a game. A separate first-party cookie, qm_visitor, lasts one day and identifies the browser for this count without changing its game identity. Visible tabs report activity once per minute. Hidden or closed tabs stop reporting and expire from the count after five minutes. We store the anonymous identifier, most recent activity time and whether this visit has been included in the total. Rows are retained for 24 hours of inactivity for deduplication, then deleted on an activity request. There is no visitor history, nickname or IP address in this table. Tabs sharing a cookie count once; different browsers or cleared cookies may count separately. Visitors blocking cookies are not counted. This is an approximate recent visitor count, not an exact number of people online or an all-time total.

Mobile app

The app does not use cookies. When you first play a ranked round, the server gives the app a signed anonymous player identifier, which the app keeps in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone and iPad, Keystore on Android) and sends with ranked games, Sudoku streaks and name reports. On your device, the app also stores your optional nickname, your last 100 saved results, your sound and appearance settings, whether you have seen the hint explanation, and your current Sudoku streak so it can resume. Deleting the app or its data removes these and gives you a new anonymous identity; it does not delete scores already on the leaderboard.

The app stores the same leaderboard and Sudoku information on the server as the website (see above). Without an internet connection you can play practice rounds, which stay on your device and are never sent to the server. The app does not count visitors, does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos, and does not access files on your device. Game sounds follow your device's silent setting.

Sharing games and results

Sharing is optional. The share message includes the game, board, result, time and whether hints or undo made it a practice round. It does not include your nickname, anonymous player identifier or session identifier. WhatsApp or your selected sharing app handles the message after you choose it. Copying keeps the text on your clipboard. Shared links open the game; they do not create leaderboard entries.

Hosting and contact messages

Microsoft Azure hosts the game and its database. Hosting services may process IP addresses, browser details, and request logs to operate and protect the service. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond and resolve your request. Please send only information needed for your enquiry.

The game page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser sends Google the network information needed to retrieve those font files, such as your IP address and browser details.

Google advertising

We have integrated Google AdSense. Ad availability depends on Google's approval and our advertising settings. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to personalise ads based on those visits, subject to your choices and applicable consent requirements.

Learn how Google uses information from partner sites. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings and find other participating vendors' opt-out choices at YourAdChoices. Browser settings also let you manage or block cookies. Where a consent message is presented, use its choices to manage advertising consent.

In the mobile app, ads are provided by Google AdMob and appear as a single banner below the game area. Because the app is played by people of all ages, every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent, ad content is limited to Google's general-audience (G) rating, and only non-personalised ads are requested. The app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and on Android it does not use the device's advertising ID. To deliver ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google receives information such as your IP address, device type and operating system, app information and ad interactions; learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law requires consent (for example in the UK and European Economic Area), Google's consent message is shown before ads load, and you can change your choice later in the app under Settings, Ad privacy choices.

Your choices and questions

You can play without supplying a nickname and manage local storage through your browser, or delete the app to remove its data from your device. To ask about your data or request removal of a leaderboard entry, use our Contact page. We may need information to identify the relevant entry. We will update this page if our practices change.

Total visits

We keep a cumulative visit count from the date this feature is enabled. A signed visitor cookie lasts 24 hours and prevents refreshes, extra tabs and game changes from adding repeated visits during that period. The same person can count again after the cookie expires or on another browser. This measures visits, not unique people. Anonymous activity records are retained for up to 24 hours of inactivity to avoid double counting, then removed on an activity request. Removing these records does not reduce the cumulative total. Browsers that block the cookie are not counted.
