Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Quiet Marble handles information when you play at quietmarblegames.com. You do not need a game account.

Game data and browser storage

A first-party anonymous player cookie connects ranked rounds to the same browser and expires after one year. Local storage keeps your last 100 saved results and sound preference. Session storage remembers your optional nickname for the tab session. You can clear these through your browser settings; the game's Privacy dialog also lets you clear local results.

Clearing browser data resets your local identity and history, but does not delete scores already submitted to the server. There is no account recovery or cross-device profile.

Public leaderboard and retention

We store an anonymous player identifier, round identifier, game, start time, optional nickname, and result metrics to validate and rank scores. Your qualifying nickname (or generated player label), moves, time, and result appear publicly. Use a nickname rather than personal information. Names are not unique or verified.

The server retains up to 100 completed results per game, with one best result per anonymous player. Superseded and lower-ranked scores are removed during cleanup. Unsubmitted rounds expire after 24 hours and are deleted on the next leaderboard read, game start, or score submission. Starting another round replaces your unfinished round for that game. A qualifying score and its nickname can remain until replaced or removed.

Sudoku streaks

For Sudoku, tab session storage keeps the current streak identifier and entered numbers so a refresh can resume your progress. The server keeps one current Sudoku streak per anonymous player, including the puzzle, starting difficulty, consecutive wins, total solving time and nickname. Inactive streaks expire after 24 hours and are deleted on the next Sudoku start, update or leaderboard request. Only the top 100 players’ best streaks are retained publicly, showing nickname, wins, total time and starting difficulty. Starting a new streak replaces current progress but preserves a qualifying best score.

Recent website visitors

The counter estimates anonymous browsers viewing the game page in the last five minutes, including visitors who have not started a game. A separate first-party cookie, qm_visitor, lasts one day and identifies the browser for this count without changing its game identity. Visible tabs report activity once per minute. Hidden or closed tabs stop reporting and expire from the count after five minutes. We store only the anonymous identifier and most recent activity time; expired rows are deleted on the next activity request. There is no visitor history, nickname or IP address in this table. Tabs sharing a cookie count once; different browsers or cleared cookies may count separately. Visitors blocking cookies are not counted. This is an approximate recent visitor count, not an exact number of people online or an all-time total.

Hosting and contact messages

Microsoft Azure hosts the game and its database. Hosting services may process IP addresses, browser details, and request logs to operate and protect the service. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond and resolve your request. Please send only information needed for your enquiry.

The game page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser sends Google the network information needed to retrieve those font files, such as your IP address and browser details.

Google advertising

We have integrated Google AdSense. Ad availability depends on Google's approval and our advertising settings. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to personalise ads based on those visits, subject to your choices and applicable consent requirements.

Learn how Google uses information from partner sites. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings and find other participating vendors' opt-out choices at YourAdChoices. Browser settings also let you manage or block cookies. Where a consent message is presented, use its choices to manage advertising consent.

Your choices and questions

You can play without supplying a nickname and manage local storage through your browser. To ask about your data or request removal of a leaderboard entry, use our Contact page. We may need information to identify the relevant entry. We will update this page if our practices change.
